Home hero — title position & using the crest

Four treatments for the top of the homepage. Each one shows the real header bar above it so the gap you are judging is the actual gap. The crest images below are the real public/logo.png at the exact sizes proposed.

Logo quality check — honest verdict first

The source file is 120 × 120 px, PNG, no transparency (the round crest sits on a white square — the site hides the corners with a circular crop, which works on any light background but would show on a dark one). The header already renders it at 52 px, which is 104 device pixels on a retina screen — it is already at ~87% of its native resolution.

Judge for yourself at the proposed sizes — these are the real file, browser-scaled exactly as the site would:

Verdict: clean enough to sit in the title lockup at 52–56 px, but not clean enough to be enlarged. Above ~64 px the ring text (“Ipswich & District Federation Bowls League”) blurs on retina displays and the crest starts to look like a scan. If a big-crest hero is ever wanted, the fix is a higher-resolution export (ideally 512 px+ with transparency) from whoever holds the original artwork.

A — same hero, tightened

No redesign — just less air. Today the kicker sits below up to 40 px of page padding plus up to 24 px of hero padding (.site-main + .page-head--home). This trims that to a flat 1rem above the kicker and removes the hero’s own top padding, pulling the title ~48 px up. One-line CSS change.

B — crest lockup

The crest at 56 px sits left of the title; the title drops one size (--xl instead of --xxl) so it holds on one line on desktop, with the kicker tucked above it. The crest is at its safe size here — compare it against the header logo above it, it should look identical in sharpness. Saves a full line of title height as well as the padding.
