League header — bringing the controls together

Problem: division letters (left) and Table/Matches (right) sit at opposite edges with dead space between. Four ways to tighten it, borrowing the competitions banner-tab feel.

Option A — Table/Matches join the banner

The view tabs move into the banner on the same row as the day tabs, right-aligned — exactly the competitions treatment. Division letters get the row below to themselves, left-aligned, close to the table they control.
