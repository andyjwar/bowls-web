v25 — match points, un-clunkied

The shots boxes work; the two tiny unlabelled PTS boxes are the clunky part. Grounding: in the real data, every saved Samford result carries points and they are always one of the fixed rink-and-aggregate combos summing to 6 (6–0, 5–1, 4–2 …), while Two Wood and Triples have never recorded points — they just score 2 for a win. Four options below; same week tile, same fixtures.

A — shots only · points live in the details editor

The row is just shots — nothing else to explain. Match points move into the players & rinks panel you already open by tapping a team name. After saving, the row shows the points quietly under the score: green when an admin entered real points, faint “auto” when standings fell back to 2–0.

B — labelled columns · points visually secondary

Same layout as today, but a small uppercase header row names the columns once per tile, so the boxes explain themselves. Points boxes go dashed-and-hollow until used — clearly optional — and turn solid when filled. Saved rows keep the same column rhythm.

C — quick-pick chips once shots are in

Rows are shots-only. The moment a row has both shot totals, a chip strip slides open under it with the only point results that exist in this league (rink points + aggregate always sum to 6): one tap and done. Every Samford result ever saved is one of these combos. “Skip” keeps the 2–0 fallback; “other” opens two boxes for the odd case.

D — per-tile reveal: ‘+ match points’

Rows are shots-only by default. A quiet toggle on the tile reveals the points column for leagues that award them — ticked once, remembered per league. Shown both ways: hidden (how Two Wood and Triples would always look) and revealed (how Samford would look every week).

Recommendation

Drive it from the league, then use C's chips where points exist. The data already answers the question D asks with a toggle: Samford records points on 100% of results, Two Wood and Triples on 0%. So leagues without points get option A's shots-only rows automatically (with the faint “auto 2–0” on saved rows), and Samford gets option C's chip strip — one tap for a result that is always one of a handful of combos, no tiny boxes, no wrong totals. Option B is the safe fallback if a new league turns up with free-form points, and its “other…” escape hatch already covers that inside C.
