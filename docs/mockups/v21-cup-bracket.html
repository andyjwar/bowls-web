Samford Knockout Cup — bracket variants

Real data. Losers faded hard, winners bold with the score in cup colour. No “Winner of Semi-final A” filler — empty slots just wait. Purple = Samford KO Cup accent.

Option A — classic bracket, one direction, with connector lines

Everything flows left to right and the connector lines make the routing self-evident: you can follow Margaret Catchpole's line from their 82–63 win into Tie 2. The final sits on the far right with date and venue. Scrolls sideways on phones.

Option B — rounds as stacked bands (pyramid)

No sideways anything: each round is a full-width band that narrows as the cup progresses, with the final centred at the bottom. Reads top to bottom like the paper sheet, works on any screen width, and the fading makes each band's winners pop. The trade-off: routing is implied by order (Tie F & Tie B feed Tie 1), not drawn.

Option C — four columns, winners carry a green “through” mark

Same four-column flow as A but without connector lines — instead every decided tie gets a small green “Through: name” underneath, so who progressed is spelled out in words. Least visual, most explicit; also the easiest to read for anyone unfamiliar with brackets.
