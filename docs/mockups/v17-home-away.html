Home & away columns

Four ways to make home/away explicit on the match list — each shown on the all-teams week tile and on the single-team filtered list. Home is always the left column.

A — column headers

A slim label strip on each tile and on the list: HOME over the left column, AWAY over the right. Rows stay exactly as they are — zero clutter inside the rows, and it doubles as a header for DATE and RESULT on the filtered list.

B — tiny H / A letters

Small muted H and A markers sit immediately beside each team name, inside the row. No headers, so nothing extra above the rows — but every row now carries two extra glyphs, and the letters compete with the W/L chips.

C — single-team venue column

The all-teams tile keeps a header strip (home/away is already positional there). The filtered list is restructured around the selected team: date | H/A chip | opponent | score | result. The team's own name disappears from every row, so each row gets shorter and the venue reads as a proper column.

D — headers + venue tint

Column headers as in A, plus in the single-team list home fixtures get a thin green left border and a green date. Home games become scannable down the page without reading anything — the tint carries the meaning, the header explains it.
