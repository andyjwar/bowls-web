v26 — points first

The public site already treats points as the headline: a played Samford row prints 5 – 1 in the green chip and relegates shots to the small print. Entry should match that hierarchy — points are what you're entering, shots are the supporting detail. Four directions, all quieter than v25. Two Wood and Triples never use points, so each option notes how it collapses to shots-only for those leagues.

A — the chip, editable: big points pair, shots tucked under

The two points boxes wear the public green chip's clothes — soft green, big 800-weight digits — and sit exactly where the printed score will appear. Shots are a small labelled pair beneath, mirroring the public “5–1” over “(65–48)”. Focus order: points, then shots, then next row.

Recommendation

A is the honest default; B is the speed play. Option A keeps the one-glance, all-rows-editable week tile the user already loves, just re-weighted so entry mirrors the printed result — green points big, grey shots small — and it degrades to today's clean two-box row for Two Wood and Triples with zero new patterns. If entry speed matters more than uniformity, B's chips make points literally one tap and remove the only mislabel-able boxes. C is elegant but gambles on free-text parsing; D reads beautifully but doubles the taps. A sensible pairing: A's layout with B's chips appearing under the focused row as a shortcut, not a requirement.
