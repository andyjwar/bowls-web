Locations in the fixture list

Upcoming fixtures are played at the home team's green, so every row already implies a location. Five ways to surface it — all clickable through to directions, all reusing the club-locations data just shipped.

Option A — postcode chip beside the home team

A small pin + postcode chip sits after the home team's name (home side only — that's where the match is). Tap opens directions. Byes and played results show no chip.

Option B — venue subline under each fixture

A quiet centred line under the teams: green name, postcode, and an arrow — the whole line is the link. Reads like FotMob's stadium line. Roomier, but nothing competes with the team names.

Option C — directions button at the row edge

The grid gains a slim fourth column: a bordered pin button that opens directions to the home green. Zero extra text — the tidiest option — with the postcode shown on hover/long-press.

Option D — tap a fixture to reveal the map

Rows stay exactly as they are today, plus a small caret. Tapping expands a panel with the Locations-page map thumbnail, green name, postcode, and Directions / Locations-page links. Most informative without permanent clutter.

Option E — fixtures grouped by green within the week

The week tile gains slim venue headers, so everyone playing at the same green sits together — handy when a club hosts two matches the same evening. The postcode on the right is the directions link.
