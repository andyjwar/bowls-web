v22 — admin redesign: score entry first

The public site now leads with leagues, cups, and “this week”. The admin page still leads with CSV import, and manual score entry is three clicks deep inside “League history”. This flips it: the primary admin job is entering a week of scores per league, with everything else behind it.

What exists today vs what the public site needs

Keeps working, just re-homed:

  • CSV import + imported-results check → becomes a secondary “Bulk import” tool inside Results.
  • League teams (club names per division) and Registered players → merge into one “Clubs & players” area.
  • Form submissions → stays, but gets an unread badge in the nav.

Genuinely missing today:

  • A week-at-a-time score entry grid (the fixture editor is one modal per match).
  • Any cup/competition editing — competitions-2026.json is hand-edited while the public site now has bracket + matches views.
  • An overview of what still needs entering, mirroring the public “This week” carousel.
A — overview landing: the entry queue

After sign-in you land on a queue, not a form. One card per league-day for the current week, driven by the same schedule data as the public day carousel: how many fixtures have scores, how many don’t. Click through to the entry grid (option B) with league, division, and week pre-selected. Cup rounds and new form submissions surface here too.

B — score entry by league, a week at a time

The core screen. Same drill-down order as the public league pages (league → day/section → division → week) so the mental model matches, but every fixture in the week is an editable row. Shots are typed, points auto-fill from the league’s rules but stay editable, and one button saves the whole week. Player names and rink detail stay in the per-match editor, opened only when needed.

C — competitions: round-by-round cup entry

Today the cup JSON is edited by hand while the public site renders brackets from it. Minimal admin: each round is a list of ties with score boxes and a walkover select; saving a round offers to fill winners into the next round’s placeholders (the from: [F, B] links already exist in the data).
