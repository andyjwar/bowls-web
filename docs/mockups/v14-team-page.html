Team pages — how they could work

Entry point first, then three ways a team's results and fixtures can live on one page.

Entry point

Team names in the table are links

Names keep their weight but act as links — hovering shows a green chevron so it's obviously clickable. Tapping goes straight to the team's page. (Match cards' team names could link the same way.)

Option A

One season timeline, "up next" divider

Everything in one chronological list: played matches at the top with scores and W/L badges, then a clear green divider, then what's still to come — the very next match outlined. No tabs, nothing to learn; you scroll the season.

Option B

Next match pinned, then two labelled sections

The next match gets a big outlined card right under the banner (that's the #1 question a player has). Below it, "Results" and "Fixtures" are separate, clearly-labelled stacks — same info as A but grouped rather than one stream.

Option C

Side-by-side columns (desktop)

Results on the left, fixtures on the right, so the whole season is visible without scrolling. Collapses to stacked sections (Option B) on phones. Densest; a bit more to take in.
